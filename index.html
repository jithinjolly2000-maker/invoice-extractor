Invoice Data Extractor

Paste raw invoice text (from a PDF copy-paste, email, or OCR) and it pulls out the structured fields — invoice number, dates, VAT numbers, currency and amounts — using pattern rules. Review, then export.

🔒 Private — runs in your browser. Text is parsed locally with regex rules; nothing is uploaded. Paste your own invoice text, or load the sample. Always eyeball the result — extraction is rule-based, not magic.
